# TradFi Markets archive

[Open the market history viewer](https://dapp.ipfund.art/#traditional-markets). The catalog identifies the unit, publisher, statistic, coverage and SHA-256 digest of each series. Dates are observation dates. SEC filings use the disclosure date and retain the reporting period. Weekends, trading suspensions and public holidays do not create synthetic prices.

Daily stock and futures bars, wholesale market quotes, on-chain reads and other observations have separate counts in the viewer. See [collection coverage, source licenses and original file checksums](../../docs/brain-tradfi-sources.md).

| Source | Measurements |
| --- | --- |
| [Taiwan Stock Exchange](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11672) | Listed shares and the [TAIEX daily OHLC index](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11755). The publisher is Taiwan Stock Exchange Corporation and the responsible agency is the Securities and Futures Bureau, Financial Supervisory Commission. The relevant Taiwan government open datasets, 2016 onward, are used under the [Open Government Data License, version 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/license). Share closes come from the public [daily share report](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11549), the [top-volume report](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11673), [foreign issuer report](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11759) and [security valuations with closing prices](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/11764). The catalog identifies the reports and covered dates for each symbol. |
| [ECB reference rates](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/data/datasets/EXR) | Published foreign-currency units per euro on reference dates. |
| [World Bank Commodity Price Data](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets) | Pink Sheet monthly averages for gold, silver, platinum, copper, aluminum and maize. Dates denote month end. |
| [U.S. Energy Information Administration](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_pri_spt_s1_d.htm) and [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/) | Cushing WTI and European Brent spot prices in USD per barrel. Federal Reserve H.10 and H.15 series supply the broad dollar index and Treasury constant-maturity yields. |
| [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) | Original NVIDIA and Tesla quarterly diluted EPS, as reported. Every value links to its filing. Separate links lead to stock-price evidence. |
| [Berkshire Hathaway SEC filings](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001067983.json) | The 13F series for Apple, American Express and Coca-Cola divide quarter-end reported position value by the reported share count. Each row links to its original information table and preserves the filing date, quarter end, raw amount and the applicable USD multiplier. These are quarterly valuations per share. |
| [SEC insider transactions](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/insider-transactions-data-sets) | Apple and NVIDIA have one actual insider-reported trade per eligible disclosure month, with its transaction date, price, share count and original filing. The price can be a reported weighted average. Months without an eligible disclosure have no observation. |
| [Chainlink Data Feeds](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/price-feeds/historical-data) | Pinned contract reads on Ethereum, Arbitrum One and Avalanche C-Chain. Daily observations read the final block before the next UTC day and retain the feed’s original update time. A record stores the read block height and hash and the round's actual timestamp and ID. An unchanged round can appear on successive sample dates. |

Each oracle feed has a network, contract address, unit and independent start date. The USD/XCU quotation retains its native unit. Equity oracle prices, spot oil benchmarks and quarterly earnings remain distinct series. The update job runs every day and retains each publisher's latest available observation.

The files prefixed with `coverage-` are updater checkpoints. A date in a checkpoint means the configured proxies were checked. Only the rows listed in the catalog count as published observations.
